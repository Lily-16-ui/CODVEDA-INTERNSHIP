# Codveda Technologies – Data Analyst Internship

This repository contains my completed task submissions from my **Data Analyst Internship at Codveda Technologies (March 2026 – April 2026)**.

The internship provided an opportunity to apply data analytics concepts to practical tasks involving data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, time-series analysis, customer analysis, and data visualisation.

The repository is organised into three levels — Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced — to reflect the progression of the assigned tasks.

## Tasks and Projects

### 1. Basic Level

**Task 1 – Stock Price Dataset Cleaning**

Worked with a stock price dataset, focusing on data preparation and cleaning to improve data quality and usability for subsequent analysis.

**Task 2 – Iris Dataset Analysis**

Performed analysis on the Iris dataset to explore the data, examine patterns, and derive insights from the observations.

### 2. Intermediate Level

**Task 1 – House Price Analysis**

Worked with housing data to investigate factors and patterns associated with house prices and derive meaningful analytical insights.

**Task 2 – Stock Time-Series Analysis**

Analysed stock market data over time, exploring trends and patterns through time-series analysis.

### 3. Advanced Level

**Task 1 – Customer Churn Analysis**

Worked on customer churn analysis to investigate customer retention patterns and identify factors associated with customer attrition.

**Task 2 – Dashboard Development**

Worked on a data visualisation dashboard to present analytical findings in an accessible and structured format.

*The dashboard link and any accompanying materials are included in the relevant task folder.*
